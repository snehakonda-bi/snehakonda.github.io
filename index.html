How I work

From a question to a trusted report

  1. Frame the questionFind out what decision the report needs to support and who will use it.
  2. Agree the measuresDefine each KPI with the data owner before building anything.
  3. Build the pipelineLand source data in Fabric and clean it through Bronze, Silver and Gold.
  4. Model the dataDesign a star schema and reusable T-SQL views.
  5. ValidateReconcile against source systems and fix issues at the root.
  6. ReleasePublish through deployment pipelines with row-level security.
  7. Document and trainWrite down definitions and limits, and train the people who use it.
